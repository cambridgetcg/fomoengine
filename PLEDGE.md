# The Shield Pledge

This tool exists to help people understand pressure in words. Understanding
needs no commercial justification, KPI, revenue target, or growth obligation.

- The public checker requires no purchase. No upsell stands between a person
  and the explanation of a pressure signal.
- The taxonomy stays CC0. Licence rights remain unchanged.
- Paid API enrolment, paid audit offers, and new checkout are retired.
- Technical limits protect shared resources and remain explicit. They do not
  become a sales funnel or a promise of unlimited hosted compute.
- Existing records, keys, cancellation and refund obligations remain intact.
- Exploration, disagreement, unfinished work and rest are all permitted. There
  is no requirement to replace financial targets with productivity targets.

The owner adopted this direction on 2026-09-23. Earlier commercial plans remain
in Git history; they are not outstanding tasks.
